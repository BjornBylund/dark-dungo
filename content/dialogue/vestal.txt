# The Vestal: twenty years behind convent walls, out in the world for the
# first time. The format is explained at the top of src/campaign/dialogue.gd.

== level 1
Ancestor: The Sister cleans the mace first, and the hands after. Practical, for a saint.
Vestal: I took my vows at the convent of Saint Ilse when I was nine. I did not leave its walls for twenty years.
Vestal: When word came of the darkness here, I was the only one who asked to go. The abbess seemed relieved.

== level 2
Vestal: The abbess sold blessings at the gate. A coin for a prayer, two for a miracle. The poor got prayers.
Vestal: I said nothing. I told myself that silence was a kind of obedience.
Ancestor: Silence is a kind of everything, Sister. That is what makes it so useful to the guilty.

== level 3
Vestal: There was a child with the sweating sickness. I prayed over her for three nights, and the light came, and it was not enough.
Vestal: Her mother thanked me. I have never understood why.
Ancestor: Because you stayed. It is the staying they remember, not the cure.

== level 4
Vestal: A man was brought before the abbess for heresy. He had said that God did not live in our gold.
Vestal: I read the charges aloud. I watched him burn. I have since come to think he was right.
Ancestor: Fire purifies, the priests say. It also simplifies. There is no arguing with ash.

== level 5
Ancestor: Tonight the Sister prays, and does not rise when the prayer is done.
Vestal: I asked for a sign down there. Anything. A warmth, a word. There was only the dark, and my own voice in it.
Vestal: And yet the wounds closed. The light came anyway. I no longer know whose it is.
Ancestor: Perhaps it was always yours, and heaven took the credit. It would not be the first time.

== level 6
Ancestor: A letter came for the Sister today, sealed in the wax of Saint Ilse.
Vestal: The abbess wants me home. The convent has heard I heal without its blessing, and does not like it.
Vestal: I have not answered. Twenty years I obeyed that seal. I am finding it hard to remember why.

== level 7
Vestal: The man I watched burn had a daughter. I found her today, selling bread in the hamlet.
Vestal: I told her who I was. I expected her to spit. She asked whether he was afraid at the end. I said no. I lied.
Ancestor: A merciful lie, Sister. You are learning the trade of the world. It is not all sin.

== level 8
Vestal: I wrote back to the abbess tonight. I said I would come home when the dark here is gone, and not before.
Vestal: Then I told her to stop selling miracles at the gate. I have never written anything so frightening.
Ancestor: Defiance looks well on you, Sister. Wear it often; it will fit better with time.

== level 9
Vestal: Today I held a dying soldier's hand, and the light came before I had finished asking.
Vestal: As if it had been waiting. As if it had always been waiting, and I had always been the one who was late.
Ancestor: Then do not keep it waiting any longer.

== level 10
Vestal: I used to think mercy was something I carried for someone else, like water from a well.
Vestal: It isn't. It's mine. I choose it each time, and each time it costs me. That is what makes it worth giving.
Ancestor: Then give it, Sister. There will be no shortage of those who need it.
