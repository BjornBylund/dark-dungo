# The Grave Robber: a sexton's child who learned to empty graves as well as
# fill them. The format is explained at the top of src/campaign/dialogue.gd.

== level 1
Grave Robber: You know what the dead have that the living don't? Patience. They'll wait while you work the lid.
Grave Robber: My father dug graves for the parish. I learned to fill them from him. The other thing I learned on my own.
Ancestor: An honest trade, spoiled by curiosity. I find I cannot judge you harshly, thief.

== level 2
Grave Robber: My first real job was a duke's crypt. Marble, gold leaf, a ring on every finger.
Grave Robber: I sold one ring to a jeweller in the next town. He died within the week. I spent the coin anyway.
Ancestor: Some treasures are buried for a reason. You were never one to ask what the reason was.

== level 3
Grave Robber: I had a partner once. Corwin. Quick hands, slow conscience. Same as me.
Grave Robber: In the vault under Saint Ilse's he took the haul and shut the slab on me. Three days in the dark with the abbot's bones.
Grave Robber: I got out. Corwin didn't expect that. He expected a good deal less of anything, after.
Ancestor: Three days in a tomb, and you came out sharper. The dark is a fine tutor.

== level 4
Grave Robber: There's a collector in the capital who wants back what I took from the duke. He sends men. They don't go home.
Grave Robber: Easier to hide out here, where everyone's half dead already. No offence.
Ancestor: None taken. I am entirely dead, and I find the company agreeable.

== level 5
Ancestor: The thief came back from the dark tonight and went straight to the churchyard. To one grave in particular.
Grave Robber: My father's. Someone opened it years ago and took the ring my mother buried with him.
Grave Robber: I know the work. I know whose work. I taught them half of it.
Ancestor: The trade takes from everyone, in the end. Even from those who practise it.

== level 6
Grave Robber: Marta ran with us before Corwin. Steady hands, no nerve. I found her in the capital, selling rings off a cart.
Grave Robber: My mother's ring was on the cart. Marta didn't know whose it was. I believed that. Mostly.
Ancestor: And you paid for it, I suspect. A thief buying back what was stolen. The world has a sense of humour.

== level 7
Ancestor: Two strangers asked about the thief at the tavern tonight. Fine boots. Poor manners.
Grave Robber: The collector's men. They found me, then. I wondered when.
Grave Robber: I'll handle it. Quietly. You won't find them in your graveyard, if that's what you're asking.
Ancestor: I was asking nothing. I own a great many graves; two more would go unnoticed.

== level 8
Grave Robber: I opened my father's grave tonight. My own hands, my own spade. First honest dig in twenty years.
Grave Robber: Put the ring back on him and filled it in. First time I've ever left anything in a grave.
Ancestor: There. Now you know what the sexton knew. Some things are worth more buried.

== level 9
Grave Robber: I used to see a churchyard and count what was in it. Now I walk past and know the names.
Grave Robber: That's a dangerous thing for a thief. You start caring who you're robbing.
Ancestor: Then perhaps you are something else now. The word escapes me. It will come.

== level 10
Grave Robber: I used to think the only thing worth keeping was what you could carry out.
Grave Robber: Now I keep this lot breathing and call it my cut. Don't tell the others. They'll want a share.
Ancestor: A thief who found something worth stealing back at last: your own life.
