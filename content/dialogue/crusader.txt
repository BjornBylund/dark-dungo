# The Crusader: a knight home from a long holy war, who finds the war
# followed. The format is explained at the top of src/campaign/dialogue.gd.

== level 1
Ancestor: The knight kneels in the ruined chapel after the first descent, as if expecting an answer.
Crusader: Twelve years I fought beneath foreign suns, for a city I never saw whole. I came home thinking the war was over.
Crusader: It followed me. Or it was waiting here all along.
Ancestor: Soldiers always believe the darkness lives somewhere else. It is a comfort, of a kind.

== level 2
Crusader: At Hesperon the walls held for a hundred days. On the hundred and first, we were told to make an example of the gate village.
Crusader: I obeyed. I told myself the order was holy because it came from holy men.
Ancestor: Obedience is fine armour, knight. It rusts from the inside.

== level 3
Crusader: My squire was a boy named Tobin. He carried my shield up the breach because I told him the light would keep him.
Crusader: An arrow found him before the light did. I have prayed for him every night since. I have stopped asking why.
Ancestor: First you learn the shape of grief. Then it learns yours.

== level 4
Crusader: A rider met me on the road home with a letter from the Order. Disbanded. Our lands seized, our names struck out.
Crusader: The men who sent us to Hesperon kept their palaces. We were the debt they would not pay.
Ancestor: Every crown is paid for by someone who never wears it. You were simply told the price late.

== level 5
Ancestor: Tonight the knight does not kneel. There is only the fire, and a long stare into it.
Crusader: I called on the light down there, and nothing came. I struck anyway. The thing died all the same.
Crusader: Perhaps the light was never mine to call. Perhaps it was only ever the swing of the sword.
Ancestor: Doubt is a blade too. Hold it by the right end.

== level 6
Crusader: Down there, among the risen, one wore a pilgrim's badge from Hesperon. The gate village sold them to travellers.
Crusader: It did not attack me. It only stood there, as if waiting for me to say something. I had nothing to say.
Ancestor: The dead are patient creditors. They will wait for an answer as long as it takes.

== level 7
Crusader: I wrote to Tobin's mother tonight. Twelve years late. I told her he was brave. That was true.
Crusader: I did not tell her I sent him up the breach. That is also true. The letter is still on the table.
Ancestor: Some truths are kept from mercy, knight, and some from cowardice. Only you know which this is.

== level 8
Ancestor: The knight burned something in the courtyard fire tonight. White cloth, a red cross. It burned quickly.
Crusader: The Order's colours. I wore them to Hesperon and home again. They meant something once. I can no longer remember what.
Crusader: The armour stays. It still turns a blade. That is honest work, at least.

== level 9
Crusader: A child in the hamlet asked me for a blessing for a sick brother. I said I had none to give.
Crusader: Then promise to come back, the child said. That, I could give.
Ancestor: Smaller vows are easier to keep, and harder to break. Choose them well.

== level 10
Crusader: I have stopped fighting for heaven. Heaven has its own soldiers, if it wants them.
Crusader: I fight for the ones on my left and my right, and for a hamlet that still lights its candles. That is crusade enough for one life.
Ancestor: A holy man would call that heresy. I have known many holy men. I would rather have you.
