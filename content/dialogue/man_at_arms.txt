# The Man-at-Arms: an old soldier who came off a bridge his company didn't.
# The format is explained at the top of src/campaign/dialogue.gd.

== level 1
Ancestor: The old soldier oils armour that has outlived three lords. It will likely outlive this one.
Man-at-Arms: I took the king's shilling at fourteen. Not for glory. The levy fed you twice a day.
Man-at-Arms: Forty years on I'm still eating soldier's bread. Tastes the same. Worse, maybe.

== level 2
Man-at-Arms: The Ninth Company. Two hundred of us at the start. Brewers' sons, thieves, one fellow who swore he was a prince.
Man-at-Arms: I knew every name. I said them in order before every battle, so I'd know who to look for after.
Ancestor: A roll call of the living, kept against the day it becomes one of the dead.

== level 3
Man-at-Arms: The bridge at Varrow. The captain was down, so the order was mine. Hold until the column crossed.
Man-at-Arms: We held. The column crossed. Of the Ninth, eleven walked off that bridge. I was one.
Ancestor: You did your duty, soldier. It is the cruellest thing anyone can say to you, and they all say it.

== level 4
Man-at-Arms: They gave me a medal for Varrow. Silver, with the king's face on it.
Man-at-Arms: I threw it in the river where the Ninth went in. Seemed only fair they had something to show for it.
Ancestor: The river kept the medal. Rivers are the only honest treasurers.

== level 5
Ancestor: The old soldier sleeps poorly at the best of times. Tonight there is no sleep at all.
Man-at-Arms: In the dream I'm on the bridge again and I give a different order. Fall back. Run. Live.
Man-at-Arms: They always shake their heads. Every one of them. Even in my sleep they won't let me spare them.
Ancestor: The dead are stubborn that way. They prefer to have mattered.

== level 6
Man-at-Arms: I've stopped saying the names before a fight. I say yours instead.
Man-at-Arms: Hold the line so the others go home. That's the whole of soldiering, in the end. Took me forty years to learn it.
Ancestor: Then hold it, old soldier. I will try to send you fewer reasons to.
