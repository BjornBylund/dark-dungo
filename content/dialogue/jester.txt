# The Jester: eleven years a mad lord's fool, still keeping the tempo. A
# recruit, so the story starts at the join and level 2. The format is
# explained at the top of src/campaign/dialogue.gd.

== join
Ancestor: Word of the victory has travelled, and brought the worst sort of company with it. A musician.
Jester: Good evening, good evening! I heard there was a war on, and that it lacked a soundtrack.
Jester: I've played for kings and for corpses. The corpses applaud less, but they never heckle.
Ancestor: Mind the blade under that lute, Jester. I expect it has a repertoire of its own.
Jester: Oh, it knows only the one song. But everyone remembers it.

== level 2
Jester: I was court fool to Lord Halloran for eleven years. Lovely man. Collected heads, mostly other people's.
Jester: The trick with a mad lord is to be madder, in a way that amuses him.
Ancestor: You survived eleven years of it. That is a performance in itself.

== level 3
Jester: One winter Halloran held a feast for his enemies. He had me play while the axe went round the table.
Jester: I kept the tempo. I've been keeping it ever since. If I stop, I'll hear the rest of that evening.
Ancestor: So the music is a wall, and you are forever building it. I know something of walls.

== level 4
Jester: I had a troupe once. My sister sang, the twins threw knives. We fled Halloran's court together, at night.
Jester: The road was not kind. I'm the last of us who still performs. The others perform for worms now.
Ancestor: And you play for them still, I think. Every song has an audience you cannot see.

== level 5
Ancestor: Tonight the lute lies silent across the fool's knees, its strings untouched.
Jester: There's a song I started the night we fled. I've never been able to finish the last verse.
Jester: Down there in the dark I heard someone humming it. Not me. Something that had been listening.
Ancestor: The dark collects what we leave unfinished. It has an excellent memory, and no taste at all.

== level 6
Jester: News from the old court. Lord Halloran is dead. Choked on a fishbone at dinner, if you can believe it.
Jester: Eleven years I planned his death in a hundred ballads, and a fish did it. I've never been so jealous of a fish.
Ancestor: Tyrants rarely die as they deserve. It is the one thing they cannot command.

== level 7
Jester: Found a throwing knife in the market today. The twins' mark on the handle. I'd know it anywhere.
Jester: The seller took it off a body on the north road, years ago. I paid twice what was asked.
Ancestor: Keep it close, fool. Every troupe needs its knife-thrower.

== level 8
Ancestor: There was singing in the tavern tonight. A voice no one in the hamlet had heard before.
Jester: My sister's part. I never sang it. It was hers. Tonight there was no one else to sing it, so.
Jester: Not as good as hers. Nobody cried, which is how I know. She always made them cry.

== level 9
Jester: Down there today the dark hummed again. A different tune this time. One I'd played for the party at the fire.
Jester: It's learning. Or it's listening. Either way, I mean to give it a better ending than it expects.
Ancestor: Careful. An audience that never leaves is still an audience that never leaves.

== level 10
Jester: I finished it. The last verse. It's about a fool who walked into the dark with friends and came out again.
Jester: Not much of a rhyme. Wonderful ending, though. Shall I play it? No? Later, then. There'll be a later.
Ancestor: For once, fool, I hope you are right.

== reflect
