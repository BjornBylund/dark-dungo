# The Flagellant: a penitent from a plague village, who bleeds so that others
# need not. A recruit, so the story starts at the join and level 2. The
# format is explained at the top of src/campaign/dialogue.gd.

== join
Ancestor: A pilgrim came up the road at dawn, leaving a trail of blood that no one asked about.
Flagellant: I was told there is suffering here. More than one place can hold.
Flagellant: Give me the front rank. Give me the worst of it. Whatever strikes me does not strike them.
Ancestor: The lash and the hook. A penitent of the old kind. Let us see what sins you have saved up.

== level 2
Flagellant: I come from Morrow, in the marsh country. When the coughing sickness came, it took the children first.
Flagellant: We prayed. The bells rang day and night. The sickness did not care for bells.
Ancestor: Disease is the most devout thing in the world. It visits every house and judges none.

== level 3
Flagellant: Father Anselm said the sickness was our sin made flesh. He said it could be paid for in pain, and showed us how.
Flagellant: I was the best student he ever had. I struck harder than any of them. I thought, if I bleed enough, it will pass us by.
Ancestor: A simple arithmetic. Pain in, mercy out. If only anyone were keeping the ledger.

== level 4
Flagellant: The sickness took Morrow anyway. Every house. Every bell ringer. Everyone who knelt beside me.
Flagellant: It left me. I have asked why every night since. The lash is the only answer that comes.
Ancestor: You were spared, and you have punished yourself for it ever since. I understand the impulse better than you know.

== level 5
Ancestor: A rider from the marsh country passed through the hamlet today, with news.
Flagellant: Father Anselm did not die in Morrow. He left in the night, with the alms box, before the end.
Flagellant: Every stroke I paid for him, he spent somewhere warm. I have been bleeding into an empty purse.
Ancestor: So the faith was false and the pain was real. Which will you keep?

== level 6
Flagellant: I still bleed. I will always bleed. But not for Anselm, and not for heaven.
Flagellant: I bleed so that the ones beside me do not. That is a debt I chose. It is the only kind worth paying.
Ancestor: Then take the front, penitent. Morrow could not be saved. These, perhaps, can.

== reflect
