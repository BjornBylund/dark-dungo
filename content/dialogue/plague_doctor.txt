# The Plague Doctor: a physician who stayed when the plague came, and learned
# from it. The format is explained at the top of src/campaign/dialogue.gd.

== level 1
Ancestor: The doctor's first act on coming back is to write. Pages of it, by candlelight, in a hand no one else can read.
Plague Doctor: When the pestilence came to the city of Lusk, the physicians fled. I stayed, because no one could stop me.
Plague Doctor: Four thousand dead that summer. I learned more from them than from any master.

== level 2
Plague Doctor: The dying are very generous with their bodies. They have no further use for them.
Plague Doctor: I tried things on them. Tinctures, bleedings, fumes. Some lived longer. Some did not. I wrote it all down.
Ancestor: A ledger of suffering, kept in the name of the living. I kept one myself, once, for other reasons.

== level 3
Plague Doctor: My teacher, Master Orrell, caught the fever in the second year. I gave him my newest tincture.
Plague Doctor: It worked, for nine hours. Then it did what the fever would have done, only faster. I wrote that down too.
Ancestor: You wept, I think, after the ink was dry. Science does not forbid it. It merely does not record it.

== level 4
Plague Doctor: The academy expelled me. Unnatural practices, they said, and burned my notes in the yard.
Plague Doctor: Two years later the plague reached the capital, and they sent for my methods. I had kept copies.
Ancestor: The righteous always come back for the heretic's notes. They simply come at night.

== level 5
Ancestor: Tonight the doctor does not write. The pen lies beside the page, and the page stays blank.
Plague Doctor: What lives down there is a disease that thinks. I cannot cut it out. I cannot even name it properly.
Plague Doctor: For the first time I do not want to understand something. I only want it gone. That frightens me more than the thing itself.
Ancestor: Welcome to my condition, doctor. Curiosity brought me here, too.

== level 6
Plague Doctor: I opened one of the things from below tonight. Its blood is the same colour as the soil in the fields.
Plague Doctor: Not a creature with a sickness. A sickness that grew a creature. The distinction matters more than I would like.
Ancestor: You have found what I found, doctor. I did not stop digging either.

== level 7
Ancestor: A rider from the academy came today. The doctor read the letter twice and put it in the fire.
Plague Doctor: The plague is back in the capital. They want my methods. Again. They do not want me.
Plague Doctor: I have sent them the notes. Every page. The dying do not care whose name is on the cure.
Ancestor: Generous. Or proud. With physicians it is hard to tell the two apart.

== level 8
Plague Doctor: I have finished Master Orrell's tincture. The one that killed him. It works now.
Plague Doctor: Twenty years late for him. In time for a fever case in the hamlet this morning.
Ancestor: The dead teach slowly, but they teach well. You were a good student, in the end.

== level 9
Plague Doctor: I have stopped writing down the ones I save. Only the ones I lose.
Plague Doctor: The list is shorter than it used to be. I check it every night to make sure.
Ancestor: A ledger of debts, kept honestly. You may yet balance it.

== level 10
Plague Doctor: Master Orrell used to say a physician's first duty is to do no harm. I have done a great deal of harm.
Plague Doctor: So I keep these fools alive, one wound at a time, and call each one a payment. The debt is large. I am patient.
Ancestor: Pay it down, then. Few of us ever get the chance.
